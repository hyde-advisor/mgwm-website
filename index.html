A Common Story

We recently worked with a couple who came to us at 61 — just four years from their target retirement date. They had no Social Security timing strategy, three unconsolidated accounts from previous employers, and no coordinated withdrawal plan. By the time we built their roadmap, they realized they had been on track to leave more than $40,000 in Social Security benefits on the table simply by claiming at the wrong time. That window had been quietly closing for years while they waited to "get around to it." We got it back. But not everyone reaches us in time.

Our Story

Built for the Climb,
Not the Corner Office

For 18 years, Bobby Goodman managed wealth for ultra-high-net-worth families at Goldman Sachs. He was good at it — but something bothered him.

The sophisticated, disciplined planning protecting the wealthiest families in the country wasn't reaching the people who needed it just as much — business owners, professionals, and families on Main Street. So in 2025, he left to build the firm he wished existed.
