Our Story

Born on a Trail Run in Millcreek Canyon

For 18 years, Bobby Goodman was good at his job at Goldman Sachs. Really good. But somewhere between the long hours, the frantic pace, and the ultra-high-net-worth clients who expected the world, he realized something was missing. He wasn't spending enough time with his family. He wasn't helping people in any way that felt meaningful. He had 18 years of experience, and he was spending it on people who needed it least.

On a Saturday morning in the spring of 2024, Bobby and his friend Cade Hyde set out on a 10-mile trail run through Millcreek Canyon. They'd been talking about the idea the whole way - somewhere between Elbow Fork and Rattlesnake Trail, between climbs. Then they hit the Overlook. Salt Lake Valley spread out below them, perfect morning light, the whole valley visible in every direction. That's where it sank in. Mountain Goat Wealth Management was born at 7,000 feet, staring down at the families they wanted to help.

The name came naturally. Their running group had always called themselves the Mountain Goats. Nimble. Agile. Comfortable on steep terrain. It felt right for a firm built to guide people through one of life's most challenging climbs. Bobby brought the Wall Street discipline. Cade brought fresh eyes and a background in analytics. Together they built the firm Bobby had always wished existed, one that brings Wall Street retirement planning to real families on Main Street.

We are fiduciaries — legally required to act in your best interest. Full stop.

Common Questions

Questions We Get Asked on First Calls

Here are the questions people almost always ask before their first meeting.

I already have a financial advisor. Why would I switch?

When did your advisor last call you proactively? If the answer is months — or you can't remember — that's worth exploring. As fiduciaries, we are legally required to act in your best interest. Not all advisors are held to that standard. A second opinion costs you nothing.

What does 'fiduciary' actually mean for me?

It means we are legally obligated to put your interests above our own — always. We cannot recommend a product because it pays us a higher commission. Every recommendation must be in your best interest. Many advisors are not bound by this standard. We are.

Do I need a certain amount invested to work with you?

We work with clients at various stages of the climb — from early-career professionals building their foundation to retirees navigating distribution. The most important thing isn't the number you bring; it's whether you're serious about a plan. Our complimentary consultation is the right place to start that conversation.

I've tried financial planning before and it didn't stick.

Static plans become obsolete the moment life changes. Our approach is dynamic — we revisit your plan at key milestones and reach out proactively when something needs to change. A plan that sits in a drawer isn't a plan. Ours are built to be lived.

How are you different from a robo-advisor or a big bank?

A robo-advisor manages a portfolio. A big-bank advisor often sells products. We build a comprehensive, personalized financial strategy — one that coordinates your investments, insurance, tax planning, and retirement income into a single plan. And we proactively manage it as your life changes.

Can you work with me if I'm not in Utah, Texas, or Wyoming?

We are currently licensed in Utah, Texas, and Wyoming and are actively pursuing additional state registrations. If you're outside those states, reach out anyway — we can let you know our timeline and keep you informed when your state becomes available.
